Paywatch scans your bank or credit card transaction history and automatically finds all the subscriptions and recurring charges you are paying for. It spots things like streaming services, gym memberships, and software plans you may have forgotten about, and tells you which ones have quietly raised their price. You give it a CSV export from your bank or a tool like Plaid, and it produces a clear summary of what you are spending each month and year on recurring bills, highlighting anything that looks abandoned or overpriced. It is for individuals and small businesses who want a quick, private, no-account-required way to audit their spending without handing their data to a third-party app.
